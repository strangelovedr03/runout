Common questions

Why do some TIDAL songs play for only 30 seconds?

TIDAL currently limits third-party apps to previews. Tap Play in TIDAL to listen to the full album in the official TIDAL app.

Apple Music says it can't access my library.

Apple Music requires an active subscription with Settings › Music › Sync Library turned on. Opening the Music app once to accept its terms can also help.

When will Spotify be available?

Spotify support is coming soon.

How do I keep the tablet's screen on?

Go to Settings › Dedicated display and turn on Keep screen on. You can also schedule night dimming there.
