Common questions

Why do some TIDAL songs play for only 30 seconds?

TIDAL currently limits third-party apps to previews. Tap Play in TIDAL to listen to the full album in the official TIDAL app.

My Apple Music library doesn't appear (“We couldn't read your collection”).

Runout reads the albums saved in your Apple Music library. On the iPad, check these in order:

  1. Open the Music app once with the same Apple ID and accept its terms if it asks. Until you do, Apple doesn't let other apps read your library. This is the most common cause.
  2. Turn on Settings › Music › Sync Library.
  3. Make sure that Apple ID has an active Apple Music subscription and albums saved to its library.
  4. Allow access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music › Runout.
  5. Back in Runout, close it completely, open it again and pull down on Collection to sync.

Why don't I see hand gestures?

Hand gestures are an experimental Runout Pro feature that uses the iPad's front camera in Ambient Mode. They need iPadOS 16 or later, so the option doesn't appear on iPads that stay on iPadOS 15, such as the iPad Air 2 or iPad mini 4. On older iPads that do run iPadOS 16 they work, but may respond more slowly; they're smoothest on iPads with an A12 Bionic chip or newer. Turn them on in Settings › Ambient mode › Hand gestures.

When will Spotify be available?

Spotify support is coming soon.

How do I keep the tablet's screen on?

With Runout Pro, go to Settings › Dedicated display and turn on Keep screen on. You can also schedule night dimming there.
