Ambient Mode

When nobody is touching it, it becomes art.

After a while, your covers take over the screen. Let them drift and throw them with a finger, show one at a time like a picture frame, or build a slowly flipping wall of covers. The background takes on the colors of your artwork, and at night everything gets calmer and dimmer.

  • Now Playing. Start an Apple Music album and Ambient Mode turns into a turntable: the record spins next to its sleeve, with play, pause and skip, until you close it.
  • Always pristine. The clock moves a little every minute so a display that's always on never burns in.

Hand gestures · Experimental · iPad & Mac

Play your music without touching the screen.

Hands covered in flour, or the screen across the room? Raise an open hand and Runout follows it. Point at an album to play it, grab covers and throw them, or skip a track with a swipe in the air.

In Ambient Mode

  1. ✋
    Wake it up

    Show an open hand for half a second and a soft pointer appears.

  2. ✋
    Play an album

    Hold your hand over a cover until the ring fills. It starts after a short countdown.

  3. 👋
    Change your mind

    Swipe sideways during the countdown to cancel.

  4. ✊
    Grab and throw

    Close your fist over a floating cover to drag it, open your hand to let it fly.

On the player

  1. ✋
    Press the buttons

    Hold your hand over previous, play/pause or next for a second.

  2. 👉
    Next track

    A quick swipe to the right.

  3. 👈
    Previous track

    A quick swipe to the left.

  4. ✊
    Pause or resume

    Hold a closed fist for a second.

Requires iPadOS 16 or later, or a Mac with Apple silicon, and Runout Pro; on older iPads gestures may respond more slowly, and iPads that stay on iPadOS 15 don't show the option. Compatibility
