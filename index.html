Features

A collection you can browse, not a list you scroll.

Built for a tablet that lives on a shelf, a desk or next to the speakers.

▥

Shelf & Gallery

Your saved albums as illuminated record spines or a cover gallery, with the tracklist one tap away.

◎

What should I play?

Spin the record and let Runout pick tonight's album from your whole collection, a shelf or your favorites.

♥

Favorites & shelves

Mark favorites and build your own shelves, like “Sunday morning” or “Dinner with friends”.

▶

Full-length playback

Apple Music albums play start to finish inside Runout. TIDAL albums open in the official TIDAL app.

☾

Dedicated display

Keep the screen on, dim it automatically at night and show a slowly drifting clock that never burns in.

≡

Your playlists

Your TIDAL playlists sit next to your albums, ready to open in TIDAL.

Services

Bring the music you already saved.

Each service keeps its own collection, and you sign in through its official authorization. Runout never sees your password.

TIDAL Available

Saved albums, playlists and previews. Full-length listening opens in the TIDAL app.

Apple Music iPad & iPhone

Your library with full-length playback inside Runout for Apple Music subscribers. Android support is coming.

Spotify Coming soon

Spotify support is on its way.

Ambient Mode

When nobody is touching it, it becomes art.

After a while, your covers drift slowly across the screen. Drag one with your finger and throw it, or tap it to play. The clock moves a little every minute so a display that's always on stays pristine.
